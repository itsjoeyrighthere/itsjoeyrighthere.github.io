The core of the design is not about adding, but about revealing. 
We strip away the non-essential to expose the structure.

## THE CODES

1. **Readymade**: Using what exists.
2. **Typography**: Text as image.
3. **Iterative**: Nothing is ever finished.

This blog serves as a living archive of these thoughts.
