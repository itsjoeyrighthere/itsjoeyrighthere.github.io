# Brutalism in Modern Design

Brutalism has experienced a remarkable renaissance in the 2020s, moving beyond its architectural origins to influence web design, fashion, and visual culture at large.

## Origins and Philosophy

Originally emerging in architecture during the 1950s-70s, brutalism emphasized raw materials, geometric forms, and honest expression of structure. This "truth to materials" philosophy has found new life in the digital realm.

## Digital Brutalism

Today's digital brutalism rejects the polished, corporate aesthetic in favor of:

- Raw, unrefined visual elements
- Exposed functionality and structure
- Bold typography and stark contrasts
- Deliberately rough edges

## Cultural Impact

This aesthetic movement represents more than style—it's a statement against homogenized, over-optimized digital experiences. It celebrates the raw and the real.
